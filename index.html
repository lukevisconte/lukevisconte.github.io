Welcome! I’m Luke Visconte, an independent author researching and writing on a wide range of topics, including econometrics, applied mathematics, and philosophy.

My research, while quite broad, tends toward an underlying fixation of mine: identifying, testing, critiquing, and creating systems that better represent an ideal reality, from mathematical models to political frameworks.

When I’m not researching or writing, I enjoy everything from attending Cincinnati Reds games to visiting art exhibits. Above all else, though, I love golf, euchre, and boating.
